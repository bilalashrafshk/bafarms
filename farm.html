Global Logistics

From Pasture to Port: Our Export Pipeline

Our entire supply chain is custom-engineered to preserve premium freshness, visual appeal, and microbiological integrity for international B2B buyers.

01. Precision Farming & Rearing

Under the lush pastures of Khurrianwala, livestock are fed custom nutritional feeds formulated without any harmful growth hormones or chemical additives. RFID tags monitor exact daily weight and physical wellness metrics.

  • Organic feed formulation and silage
  • Non-GMO pasture grazing cycles
  • Constant RFID biometric health monitoring
100% Hormone-Free

02. Bio-Security Quarantine & Screening

Prior to processing, animals undergo strict isolation and comprehensive screening inside our advanced diagnostic facility to ensure they meet the regulatory mandates of importing countries.

  • FDA/OIE compliant medical clearance
  • 14-day absolute observation period
  • Complete veterinary trace log generation
0% Pathogen Rate

03. Partner Processing & Slaughter

Our feedlot cattle are processed at our partner's export-certified, state-of-the-art facility — built with grade-316 stainless steel, strict cold-chain ambient environments, computerized cutting, and automated sanitization loops meeting international export standards.

  • Partner-operated grade-316 stainless steel facility
  • Temp-controlled cutting halls (0°C to 2°C)
  • Halal slaughter certified by Shariah authorities
0-2°C Ambient Temp

04. Advanced Vacuum & Skin Packaging

To maximize shelf-life, prevent freezer burns, and maintain pristine beef juices, we utilize vacuum skin packaging (VSP) technology under clean-room specifications.

  • Multi-layer high-barrier vacuum skin packaging
  • Freshness preservation without preservatives
  • Custom label barcoding for global retail-readiness
99% Vacuum Seal Integrity

05. Uninterrupted Cold Chain Shipping

From Faisalabad to Karachi port and onto international shipping lanes, your cargo resides in state-of-the-art reefers equipped with satellite temperature-tracking sensors.

  • Real-time satellite temperature telemetry
  • Insulated shockproof shipping crates
  • Customs pre-clearance and expedited port handling
-18°C Frozen Logistics
