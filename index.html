Uninterrupted Cold Chain

Our logistics operations feature an unbroken cold-chain environment. Processed meat is chilled immediately, blast frozen (if required), vacuum packed, and loaded directly into satellite-monitored refrigerated containers (reefers) or air cargo containers.

Consignments are routed via cold-chain reefer transit to the Port of Karachi (QICT/KICT) for sea freight, or dispatched via direct air cargo channels from Faisalabad (LYP) and Lahore (LHE) international airport hubs for rapid fresh deliveries.
